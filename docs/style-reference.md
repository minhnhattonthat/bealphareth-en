# Style reference

Bealphareth: dark European fantasy, adventurers in a lawless walled town over a ruin that eats people. Plain, spoken English with bite; no modern slang beyond "okay", no anime calques. Every speaker must be recognisable with the plate covered.

## Voices

| Speaker | JP markers | English devices |
|---|---|---|
| Ash | おれ, 〜よ, 〜かな, 〜んだ | Plain country boy, gentle, unsure: "I guess", "y'know", short simple words. Never rough. |
| Arthur | 僕, 〜だ, 〜しよう, stammers in shock | Earnest young knight: full sentences, "we have to", "that's not right!". Idealist, a touch formal. "Wh-what...?" |
| Leia | 私, 〜だ, 〜のだ, 〜ねば, お前 | Stiff, terse, proud knight: no contractions, "I must", "Very well", "You need not". Never soft until she has reason. |
| Eleanor | 私, です/ます, 〜ですね, 〜下さい | Refined noble lady: no contractions, "I would be glad to", "please", "if I may". Quiet grief under it. |
| Pasca | オレ, 〜ねえ, 〜ぜ, 〜じゃねえか, 〜やがる | Rough hunter: "ain't", "gonna", "damn", dropped subjects. Blunt, warm under it. |
| Scharn | あたし, 〜だよ, 〜もん, 〜ね! | Bubbly girl: exclamation marks, "you know!", "Let's do our best!", "Come on!". Sincere, never sarcastic. |
| Jessica | あたし, あんた, 〜じゃん-like 〜だよ, 〜なの? | Sassy street thief: "Geez", "Huh?", "you know that?", digs at the listener. Fast, cocky. |
| Orpheus | 私/僕, です/ます, 〜ですけどね, ご冗談 | Silky, ironic politeness: "I daresay", "how charming", "if you insist". Compliments that sting. Flirts with every girl. |
| Eve | あたし, あなた, 〜わね, 〜わ, 〜のよ | Cool, languid, amused: "I suppose", "isn't it", "you know, dear", "well, whatever". Nothing surprises her. |
| Dias | 俺, 貴様, 〜だ, 〜か, 〜せん, 〜な | Cold and clipped, slightly archaic: "Hmph.", "Spare me.", "I care not.", few words, no contractions. |
| Noel | あたし, あなた, 〜わよ, 〜わ, 〜でしょ | Prickly bookish girl: "Honestly!", "Don't touch that!", superior, and flustered when teased ("Wh-what!? It's nothing!"). |
| Luca | ぼく, 〜です, stutters (さ、先程の) | Timid monk: very polite, hedging, stammer on the first letter: "Th-that is...", "I-if it's no trouble". |

| Valdes | 俺, 〜ねえ, 〜だ, 〜ぜ, お前 | Big-brother warmth in rough speech: "ain't", "kid", "you take care now". Awkward making speeches. |
| Eugen | 俺, 〜ねえ, 〜だな, 〜ぜ, 〜よ | Gruff old trader: "ain't", "gotta", "fella", grumbles, "Takes me back". |
| Tiara | 私, です/ます, 〜下さい, 〜でしょうか | Gentle young priest, soft and prayerful: "please", "may ... ", never contractions in prayer. |
| Kadmon | オレ様, お前ら, 〜ねえ, 〜だろ | Greedy thug in a cassock, brags in the third person: "the great Kadmon", "Heh", "ya", "pal". |
| Sadiya | 私, です/ます | Calm, measured, formal; no wasted words, no contractions. Warmth only for Fatum and Nada. |
| Fatum | 俺, 奴, 〜か, 〜な, 〜だ | Bitter, weary, cutting: "How absurd.", "Heh.", short sentences. |
| Gaius | 貴様, 〜ぬ, 〜か, 愚か者, くっくっ | Slow archaic menace: "Fool.", "Heh heh...", "Would you not?", enjoys killing. |
| Avadail | 私, 君, 〜だ, 〜がいい | Serene teacher, unhurried, kindly terrible: "Come, young one.", calls the hero "you". No contractions. |
| Barkail, other Saints | 我, 〜ぬ, 〜なり | Old and scriptural: "We are born of God.", "Be not afraid", no contractions, no "thee/thou" outside scripture quotes. |
| Scripture and inscriptions (『我は氷の封印なり。...』) | 〜なり, 〜せよ | Biblical cadence: "I am the seal of ice. Seeker of the way, show thy power of ice." |
| Hero, player choices | varies | Short and neutral; male or female, so no gendered words unless the entry says so. |
| Rough NPCs (ruffians, drunks) | 〜ねえ, 〜やがる, おめえ | "ain't", "ya", insults. Knights and clergy speak formally. |
| Katakana-only lines (spirits, a strange voice: アリガトウ) | whole sentence in katakana | ALL CAPITALS, short: "THANK YOU." |

## Paired examples (the approved pilot, `RES#1023`)

JP: `ビアストクか・・・ / 遠い所からご苦労なことだな。 // ここに来るのに2ヶ月はかかったろ? / まあ、最低2ヶ月は生き延びろよ。 / じゃねえと、笑い話にも / ならねえからな。`
EN: `Biastok, huh... A long haul you made of it. // Must've taken you two months to get here, right? So stay alive two months at least. Otherwise it ain't even funny.`

JP: `この町は、貧乏人が / 命の危機の代償に富を掴む場所だ。 / 貴族が遊びで来る所じゃねえ。`
EN: `This town is where paupers trade their lives for a shot at riches. It ain't a place nobles come to play.`

JP: `本当は『太陽帝国』の歴史から / 説明したかったのですが、 / 財宝にしか興味のない人の方が / 多いでしょうから今度にしましょう。`
EN: `I would have liked to begin with the history of the 'Sun Empire', but most of you care only for treasure, so that can wait for another day.`

JP: `んーと、あまり注目されて喋るのは / 得意じゃねえんだが・・・`
EN: `Uh, I ain't too good at talking with everyone staring...`

JP: `『自由』なんてものは、 / そいつの力に見合った分しか / [5]ないって事を覚えとけ!!`
EN: `Remember, you only get as much 'freedom' as [5]you've got the strength to back up!!`

Corrected: `・・・全然そうは見えねえがな` was first "But you don't look like that at all"; flat. Approved: `...You sure don't look it.`

Keep ` // ` page breaks where the Japanese has them and write across them as one utterance. Refrains repeat word for word: `で、身分は? これもきまりでな。` is always `So, your station? Rules are rules.`
